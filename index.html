The Hands Behind Your Crown

Hello, I’m Liz.

I'm the girl behind Styled by Liz, and I love helping women feel beautiful and confident through their hair. For me, it's not just about getting your braids done. It's about the little details, the neat partings, and making sure you leave happy with your look.

I know how important it is to feel comfortable while getting your hair done, so I always do my best to make every appointment a good experience. Whether you come to me in Kutus or I come to you, you'll get my full attention and care from start to finish.

My goal is simple: to give you beautiful, neat hairstyles you'll love, while making your hair appointment as comfortable and stress-free as possible.

Before You Book

A few simple things that help your appointment go smoothly.

Booking Terms

  • check_circlePrices on this site are for labour only.
  • check_circleWant it all-inclusive, with the hair included? A deposit is needed to secure your booking. I’ll share the details on WhatsApp.
  • check_circleIf you’ve paid a deposit and cancel within 10 hours to your appointment time, you’ll lose half of your deposit, unless you choose to reschedule instead.
  • check_circlePlease arrive on time. If you’re more than 25 minutes late and haven’t let me know, we’ll need to reschedule your appointment. If you’re running behind, message me on WhatsApp.
  • check_circleFor home visits, the travel fee depends on your location.

Appointment Etiquette

  • favoriteMy studio is in my home, so I kindly ask you to come on your own. If your situation needs someone with you, just let me know when you book.
  • favoriteBefore your appointment, tell me your hair type and, if you can, send me a photo of your hair on WhatsApp so I can prepare properly.
